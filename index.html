The Social Circle Format

A fun, mixed doubles pickleball social. Designed to be highly structured, engaging, and friendly.

  • Mixed Doubles: Teams play mixed setups.
  • 4 Courts: 4 separate matches run concurrently on 4 courts.
  • 20 Rounds: Players rotate schedules across 20 rounds of play.

Scoring Protocols

The event is run with Rally Scoring which speeds up game progression:

  • Rally Scoring: Every single rally awards 1 point, regardless of which team served.
  • Target: The configured target score applies to every court.
  • Winning margin: The configured “win by” rule is enforced for standard results.

TT Service Rotation Rule

The serving side rotates predictably every 2 total points scored, no matter who wins the points:

  • Team A serves for the first 2 total points (Scores: 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, etc.).
  • Team B serves for the next 2 points.
  • The rotation alternates blocks of 2 serves continuously (A, A, B, B, A, A, B, B).
  • Calculated as: Math.floor((Score A + Score B) / 2) % 2.

Time Limit Termination

The configured match countdown is shown on every referee panel:

  • The timer can start automatically with the first point or manually.
  • Upon timer expiry ("TIME UP"), referees finalize the ongoing rally.
  • If a team is leading, the referee can finalize the current score directly.
  • If tied at timer expiry, players must perform one deciding rally to resolve the tie. No tie scores are allowed.
